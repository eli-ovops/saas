Clear product boundaries

Decision support.
Not delegated authority.

ClaimFlow helps teams find, organize and evaluate commercial evidence. Accountability stays with the contractor.

01Does not replace legal counsel

Contract interpretations and claim strategy require qualified professional judgment.

02Does not submit claims automatically

A named human reviewer must approve every export and customer submission.

03Does not approve change orders

Only authorized project parties can approve scope, price or entitlement.

04Does not replace the project manager

The PM validates facts, costs, causation and commercial context.

05Does not guarantee recovery

Recoverability depends on contract terms, facts, timing, evidence and counterparty decisions.
